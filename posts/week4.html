This week we zoom out from individual philosophers and ask what happens when connections organize themselves into communities — and what weighted links and backbones reveal that the full network hides.

Meet the philosophers.

Everyone on Wikipedia's seven lists of philosophers born before the 20th century. A directed edge means that one philosopher's article links to another. The edge weight counts how many times that link appears. For this week's analysis we collapse the two directions by summing their weights.
